    Footprint: each candle split into price rows — left column = market sells (hit the bid), right column = market buys (lifted the ask), in MT5 lots (or ounces — Settings), candle in the middle. Darker colour = more volume. Strong red/green cell with bold number = imbalance (one side 3x the other, diagonally). Grey row = busiest price of the candle (POC). Under each candle: sells / buys totals and a box with Delta (buys − sells) and Total. Candle sizes 1 min – 4 hours; the server sends the full history (30 days for 4h), 4h candles start at MT5 server times.

    Colours: Settings → Colours changes candles, buys/sells, strong cells, POC, value area, walls, accent, background, grid and text (Reset colours = back to normal); each value area has its own colour in Value areas ▾; the heatmap colours are under Heatmap → Style.

    Value Area tab: candles with as many value areas as you like (Value areas ▾): previous day, today, this week, last week (MT5 days) or a custom range (tap its first and last candle). Each one shows the volume per price from its left edge, the delta (buys − sells, green/red) per price from its right edge, the value area (70% of the volume) shaded and the POC line; "extend" carries the lines to the right like TradingView. Live absorption (pulsing bubble + ABSORB tag on the price scale + alert): in the last minute unusually heavy selling (buying) keeps hitting the low (high) and price will not go through — it is being absorbed right now; afterwards a ✓ (held: price moved $1 away) or ✕ (broke through). Absorption bubbles: a swing high/low where unusually much traded right at the extreme and price then turned away — red at highs (buying absorbed, bearish), green at lows (selling absorbed, bullish), bigger = more volume, dashed = still forming (confirms within about 2–3 candles).

    Right panel: today's volume profile (Tirana day): yellow = fair price (POC), blue dashed = value area (70%) — lines are drawn behind the numbers.

    ABS ▲ = selling absorbed at the low (heavy selling, price closed well off the low) · ABS ▼ = buying absorbed at the high · side bars = 3+ stacked imbalances. ★ = at the fair price / value area edge.

    Order walls (shaded zones): prices where unusually big buy (blue) or sell (orange) orders are waiting in the order book and have stayed there for a while — price often stalls or bounces there; if a wall suddenly disappears, it was probably fake. Our server records the order book 24/7, so walls and the heatmap are already filled in (last 2 hours) when you open the app — see the Server tab.
Heatmap: waiting limit orders (brighter = bigger) over time, white line = price, dots = aggressive buys (green) / sells (red) per second.

    Exchanges: by default the footprint, value areas, delta and live absorption add the gold perpetual trades of Bybit, OKX, Bitget, Gate and MEXC to Binance's (prices moved onto Binance's by their measured gap) — about 70% more volume. Candle prices and the order book stay Binance's. Settings → Exchanges → Binance only switches it off.

    Top line: US 10-year yield and dollar index (green = falling = usually good for gold, red = rising), the options gamma regime (gamma− = moves tend to run, gamma+ = tend to calm down) and the next US news. News lines (red = high impact, orange = medium) mark the events on the charts. Options walls: CALL wall (often caps rallies), PUT wall (often supports dips), GAMMA FLIP (above it moves calm down, below they run) — from GLD options, refreshed every 15 min in US hours.

    MT5 prices: with the GoldPriceBridge EA running in your MT5, the app takes the price gap straight from your MT5 (exact); without it, from Binance's spot-gold index.

    Data: Binance gold perpetual (real exchange trades and order book). Its price is close to, not equal to, your MT5 XAUUSD: by default the app converts to MT5 prices automatically while the gold market is open (from Binance's spot-gold index) and freezes the gap on weekends. Drag the chart to move it. Drag the price scale (right edge) up/down to make candles taller/shorter, drag the time strip (bottom) left/right to make them wider/narrower — like MT5. When you zoom out, rows are merged into bigger round $ rows (e.g. $0.50 → $2.50, shown as "rows $2.5 auto" at the top) so the numbers stay readable; zoom in for the fine rows again (Settings → Bigger rows when zoomed out). Double-tap the price scale to reset. LIVE = follow the price.
