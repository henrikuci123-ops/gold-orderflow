    Footprint: each candle split into price rows — left column = market sells (hit the bid), right column = market buys (lifted the ask), in MT5 lots (or ounces — Settings), candle in the middle. Darker colour = more volume. Strong red/green cell with bold number = imbalance (one side 3x the other, diagonally). Grey row = busiest price of the candle (POC). Under each candle: sells / buys totals and a box with Delta (buys − sells) and Total. Candle sizes 1 min – 4 hours; the server sends the full history (30 days for 4h), 4h candles start at MT5 server times.

    Right panel: today's volume profile (Tirana day): yellow = fair price (POC), blue dashed = value area (70%).

    ABS ▲ = selling absorbed at the low (heavy selling, price closed well off the low) · ABS ▼ = buying absorbed at the high · side bars = 3+ stacked imbalances. ★ = at the fair price / value area edge.

    Order walls (shaded zones): prices where unusually big buy (green) or sell (red) orders are waiting in the order book and have stayed there for a while — price often stalls or bounces there; if a wall suddenly disappears, it was probably fake. Our server records the order book 24/7, so walls and the heatmap are already filled in (last 2 hours) when you open the app — see the Server tab.
Heatmap: waiting limit orders (brighter = bigger) over time, white line = price, dots = aggressive buys (green) / sells (red) per second.

    Data: Binance gold perpetual (real exchange trades and order book). Its price is close to, not equal to, your MT5 XAUUSD: by default the app converts to MT5 prices automatically while the gold market is open (from Binance's spot-gold index) and freezes the gap on weekends. Drag the chart to move it. Drag the price scale (right edge) up/down to make candles taller/shorter, drag the time strip (bottom) left/right to make them wider/narrower — like MT5. Double-tap the price scale to reset. LIVE = follow the price.
